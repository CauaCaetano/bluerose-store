The firm

A full-service structure for decisions that involve more than one area of law.

Founded in 1994, Bastos Lacerda brings together lawyers from nine practice areas who work as one team. A corporate reorganization, for instance, usually also raises tax, labor and contractual questions, and the team is built around that reading.

We work with privately held companies, family groups, investor-backed companies and organizations in regulated sectors, both on advisory matters and in disputes.

Sectors

Sector knowledge

Rules, regulators and typical contracts change from one sector to another. Our work is also organized by economic sector.

All sectors →

Agribusiness

Growers, cooperatives, trading companies and agro-industries deal with rural credit, crop contracts, land, environmental matters and family succession at the same time.

Industry

Industrial plants concentrate long-term supply contracts, large workforces, significant tax credits and safety and environmental requirements.

Technology

Software companies, platforms and digital services grow with investment, process large amounts of data and depend on well-protected intellectual property.

Financial markets

Asset managers, fintechs, securitization companies and issuers in the capital markets operate under detailed regulation and constant supervision.

Infrastructure and energy

Generation, transmission, sanitation and logistics projects involve long-term contracts, concessions, permits and relations with regulatory agencies.

Real estate

Developers, investors, retail chains and logistics operators need legal certainty when acquiring, using and exploiting real estate.

Retail and services

Brick-and-mortar and online retail, franchises and service companies deal with consumers, brands, customer service teams and many daily transactions.

Healthcare

Hospitals, clinics, laboratories, health plans and manufacturers work with sensitive data, health regulation and contracts with many parties.
