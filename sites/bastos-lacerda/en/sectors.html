Sector

Agribusiness

Growers, cooperatives, trading companies and agro-industries deal with rural credit, crop contracts, land, environmental matters and family succession at the same time.

Talk to the sector group →Portuguese page

Legal characteristics

  • Financing through CPR, CDA/WA and Fiagro funds
  • Crop purchase, partnership and rural lease agreements
  • Family rural assets and succession planning
  • Environmental requirements linked to credit and exports

Regulatory topics

  • Rural Product Note (Law 8,929/1994)
  • Forest Code and Rural Environmental Registry
  • Land Statute: rural leases and partnerships
  • Taxation of rural activities and tax reform
  • Acquisition of land by foreign investors

Related practice areas: Tax, Commercial contracts, Corporate and M&A, Real estate.

Sector

Industry

Industrial plants concentrate long-term supply contracts, large workforces, significant tax credits and safety and environmental requirements.

Talk to the sector group →Portuguese page

Legal characteristics

  • Supply chains with long-term contracts
  • Operations involving IPI, ICMS and special regimes
  • Large workforces and occupational safety standards
  • Environmental licensing of facilities

Regulatory topics

  • Regulatory standards (NR-1, NR-12 and others)
  • Environmental licensing
  • Tax incentives and the tax reform transition
  • Antitrust (CADE)
  • Foreign trade and customs regimes

Related practice areas: Tax, Labor and employment, Commercial contracts, Compliance and investigations.

Sector

Technology

Software companies, platforms and digital services grow with investment, process large amounts of data and depend on well-protected intellectual property.

Talk to the sector group →Portuguese page

Legal characteristics

  • Investment rounds and stock option plans
  • Software as the company's main asset
  • Large volumes of personal data
  • Licensing, SaaS and service-level agreements

Regulatory topics

  • General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018)
  • Brazilian Internet Civil Framework (Law 12,965/2014)
  • Startup legal framework (Complementary Law 182/2021)
  • Software Law (Law 9,609/1998)
  • Regulatory debate on artificial intelligence

Related practice areas: Data protection, Intellectual property, Corporate and M&A, Commercial contracts.

Infrastructure and energy

Generation, transmission, sanitation and logistics projects involve long-term contracts, concessions, permits and relations with regulatory agencies.

Talk to the sector group →Portuguese page

Legal characteristics

  • Construction and equipment supply contracts
  • Concessions and public-private partnerships
  • Environmental licensing of projects
  • Project finance and collateral

Regulatory topics

  • ANEEL and power sector regulation
  • Concessions Law (Law 8,987/1995) and PPPs (Law 11,079/2004)

Sector

Real estate

Developers, investors, retail chains and logistics operators need legal certainty when acquiring, using and exploiting real estate.

Talk to the sector group →Portuguese page

Legal characteristics

  • Acquisitions with title and seller due diligence
  • Long-term commercial leases
  • Development, segregated assets and off-plan sales
  • Zoning and environmental regularization

Regulatory topics

  • Tenancy Law (Law 8,245/1991)
  • Real Estate Development Law (Law 4,591/1964)
  • Property registry and Law 14,382/2022
  • Fiduciary lien on real estate (Law 9,514/1997)
  • Master plans and zoning

Related practice areas: Real estate, Commercial contracts, Tax, Litigation and arbitration.
