Ask on WhatsApp
Is a small scam worth pursuing?

Often the fastest path is the bank dispute process, which we guide you through in the free triage. When the bank failed, smaller amounts can go to small claims court.

Do you work outside Florida?

Yes. Consultations are online and we work with co-counsel where a local license is required.

How much is a consultation?

The first triage is free. After that you get a flat-fee proposal in writing before anything is signed.

Do screenshots count as evidence?

They help, but can be challenged. We capture evidence with forensic tools that hold up in court.

How fast can content come down?

With platform notices, often in days. With a court order, usually one to three weeks.
