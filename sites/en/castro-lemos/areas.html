Practice areas

Digital law, end to end

We represent people and businesses on six fronts. In all of them, the starting point is the same: understand fast and protect the evidence.

01 · Payment app scams

Money sent to the wrong person?

Fake support agents, family-emergency scams, invoice fraud and purchases that never arrived. We pursue recovery through the bank, freeze the receiving account and, when the bank failed, bring a claim.

  • Bank dispute and recall requests
  • Freezing the receiving account
  • Claims against the financial institution
  • Damages when available

02 · Cybercrime

Hacked accounts, impersonation and threats

We represent victims of hacking, online fraud, harassment, stalking and defamation.

  • Account and profile recovery
  • Identifying the author by subpoena
  • Coordinating with law enforcement

03 · Data privacy

For businesses that hold customer data

From assessment to a working privacy program. And if a breach already happened, a coordinated response: notices to customers and regulators and defense in the inquiry.

  • Data mapping and risk assessment
  • Privacy policies, contracts and terms
  • Breach response
  • Outsourced privacy officer
