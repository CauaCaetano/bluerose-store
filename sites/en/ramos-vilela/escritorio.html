The firm

Founded in 1987, built for today

A firm built client by client, with counseling and litigation across the country.

Our path

From an employment boutique to full service

In the 1990s, client demand led to corporate and tax practices. In 2008 we opened in New York and in 2015 in Washington, focused on federal and appellate matters.

In 2022 we launched a privacy and compliance practice, now one of the fastest growing.

Clients do not need an opinion; they need a decision they can trust.

Cecilia Vilela, founding partner
