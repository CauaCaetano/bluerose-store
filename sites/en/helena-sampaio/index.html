FAQ

Common questions before a consultation

Is the first consultation free?

The first conversation on WhatsApp, to understand your case, is free. A full consultation with document review has a flat fee, shared before booking.

Do you take cases outside Newark?

Yes, anywhere in New Jersey, and remote consultations for many matters.

How will I know what is happening?

You get updates by WhatsApp whenever something important moves, and you can reach me any time.

How long does a divorce take?

Uncontested divorces can finish in a few months. Contested cases depend on the court calendar.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Payment terms are agreed in writing before work begins.

Do you handle emergencies like arrests?

Yes. For urgent criminal matters, message me at any hour.
