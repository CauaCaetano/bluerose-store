Questions we get

About Atelier Serena

Which blonde technique is right for my hair?

It depends on your natural base, color history and the result you want. That is why every first visit starts with a free color consultation.

Where to get hair extensions in Beverly Hills?

At Atelier Serena, one block from Rodeo Drive. We use human hair and three application methods.

What is the space like?

A restored villa with natural light, well-spaced chairs and our own bistro. Ideal when you will spend a few hours with us.

Do you offer private service?

Yes. A private suite for brides, public figures and clients who prefer discretion, booked in advance.
