Questions

Before you start laser

How many sessions will I need?

Usually 8 to 10, spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart. The exact number depends on the area, hair type and skin type.

Does it hurt?

Our diode laser has a cooled tip. Most people feel only mild warmth.

Can darker skin be treated?

Yes. Our device is cleared for all skin types.

Can I go in the sun?

Avoid strong sun on the area two weeks before and after each session.
