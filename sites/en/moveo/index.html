Who we are

One-on-one care, from evaluation to discharge

Moveo opened in 2014 with a simple rule: every session is with one therapist, start to finish, never split between patients.

We combine manual therapy, therapeutic exercise and technology to treat the cause of pain, not just the symptom.

  • Mission: restore movement and independence
  • Vision: Central Florida’s reference in rehab
  • Values: listening, science and respect for your pace
