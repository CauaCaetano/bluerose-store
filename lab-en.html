Run the automations right here.

The real Python scripts from the portfolio, running in your browser. Use your own data: nothing is installed and nothing leaves your computer. The scripts answer in English here and in Portuguese on the Portuguese page.

Paste a sales CSV (columns date,product,category,amount) or pick a file. The script totals by category and month and writes resumo.csv.

A messy folder becomes subfolders by type. Type file names or pick files from your computer: only the names are read, the content never leaves this page.

Real sending is isolated in the enviar_mensagem_whatsapp function: in production it calls the official WhatsApp API.

The customer generates a PIX payment and disappears. After the waiting time, the script checks the payment and only sends a reminder if it is still pending.
