1. Who we are and what these terms cover

BLUE ROSE builds websites, customer-service automations (such as WhatsApp) and custom systems for small businesses. These terms apply to quotes, proposals and services hired through this website, WhatsApp or email.

Every project has a written proposal with scope, price, timeline and deliverables. If the proposal differs from these terms, the proposal prevails. The provider's full identification (legal name, tax ID and address) is included in the proposal, sent before any payment.

2. Quotes and proposals

  • Prices are fixed: you know the total before we start, with no hourly billing inside the agreed scope.
  • Prices on the pricing page and in “Build your own” are references. The proposal price is the one that applies.
  • Proposals are valid for 15 days.
  • Anything outside the approved scope (new pages, features, languages and so on) is quoted separately and only done after your written approval.

3. Hiring and payment

  • You hire us when you approve the proposal in writing (WhatsApp or email count) and pay the deposit.
  • Standard payment: 50% to start and 50% on delivery, unless the proposal says otherwise.
  • The website goes live on your domain and access is handed over after full payment. Until then, the preview stays at a test address.
  • Monthly services (such as the care plan) may be paused after 30 days of late payment, with prior notice.

4. Timelines

  • Timelines are counted in business days from the deposit and the receipt of all required material (copy, photos, logo, prices, access).
  • If material or an approval is late, the timeline is paused for the same period.
  • If a project stalls for more than 30 days waiting on you, we notify you in writing. With no reply within 10 days, the project may be closed as is, billed in proportion to the work done.

5. Revisions and changes

  • Each plan includes the revision rounds listed in the proposal. A round is one list of changes, sent at once, within the approved scope.
  • After the included rounds, changes cost $40 per hour (quoted in advance) or are covered by the care plan, when hired.
  • Scope changes are not revisions: they become a new quote.

  • Keep the passwords and access you receive safe, and change them after delivery.

You are responsible for third-party claims caused by the content or information you provided.

7. WhatsApp automations and messages

  • Automations use the official WhatsApp API (Meta) or the tools named in the proposal, and follow those providers' policies.

  • You approve what the automation may say and do before it goes live, and you are responsible for the content sent to your customers.
  • Number bans, suspensions, price or policy changes by Meta or other providers are outside our control. When they happen, we help within the hired scope.
