1. Who handles your data

BLUE ROSE is the controller of the personal data processed by this website (bluerose-store.com). To ask about your data, use the contact form or reply to any message from us.

2. What we collect

  • Contact form: name, business name, phone (optional), interests, message, the page and language you wrote from, the link code that brought you (if any) and a record that you agreed to this policy and the Terms (with date and version).
  • Visit counts on the example websites: action type (visit, simulation or “I want a site like this” click), which example, which page and the language. We do not store names, IP addresses, cookies or anything that identifies you.
  • Preferences in your browser: your language and the name, colors and logo used in the live preview. They stay only on your device (browser local storage) and never reach us.
  • Lab and Nexo: the scripts and the demo run in your browser. What you type or upload there never leaves your computer.
  • When you become a client: proposal and contract data (identification, contact, billing and business details).

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to you and prepare a quote or proposal, at your request.
  • To deliver the hired service and bill for it.
  • To understand which examples interest people most and improve the website, using anonymous data.
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising and we do not sell data.

4. Who we share it with

  • Supabase: the database that stores contact form entries and visit counts, with servers in São Paulo, Brazil.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website and logs technical access data (such as IP) for security, under GitHub's policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude): helps us prepare replies and proposals from what you wrote to us.
  • Meta (WhatsApp): when we talk to you on WhatsApp.
  • Content providers: example images (Unsplash) and the Lab's Python (jsDelivr) load from those services, which receive technical connection data.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

5. International transfers

Some providers (such as GitHub, Anthropic and Meta) may process data outside the country where you live. They use contractual safeguards and security measures for these transfers.

6. How long we keep it

  • Contacts that did not become clients: up to 12 months after the last conversation, then deleted or anonymized.
  • Clients: for the contract term and the legal periods after it (such as tax obligations).
  • Visit counts: they identify no one and may be kept for statistics.

7. Your rights

You may ask at any time to access, correct, delete or export your data, to know who we share it with, and to withdraw consent. We reply within 15 days. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

8. Security

The contact form writes to a protected database where the public can only insert a new request, never read, change or delete anything. Private access keys are never placed in the website code. Internal access is limited to the people handling your request.

9. Cookies

This website uses no tracking or advertising cookies. We only use your browser's local storage to remember preferences (such as language and the live preview), and you can clear it in your browser settings whenever you want.

10. Audience

Our services are meant for businesses and people over 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the current version. Important changes are announced on this website.
